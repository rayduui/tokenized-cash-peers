DTCC expands eligible collateral for tokenized MMFs

DTCC · 20 Nov 2026

Updated schedule recognises selected tokenized USD money-market funds as eligible margin for certain cleared products, with published haircuts of 2–5% depending on structure and transfer-agent controls.

Implication. Improves booking and margin efficiency for global cash books.

Securitize expands BUIDL dealing capacity

BlackRock / Securitize · 18 Nov 2026

Transfer agent increased daily capacity. Settlement remains T+0 against USDC in the existing 24/7 window for eligible institutions.

Implication. Higher operational throughput for large flows.

ISDA guidance on tokenized-fund CSA treatment

ISDA · 19 Nov 2026

White paper clarifies how tokenized short-term government and money-market shares may be treated under existing CSA language when the underlying is eligible.

Implication. Reduces legal friction for bilateral collateral use.
